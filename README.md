## My Development Approach

I do not force every project into the same theme or builder. I choose the approach based on the project's design, functionality, performance requirements, and long-term maintainability.

**WordPress** is the foundation.

**Elementor Pro** is my primary visual development tool for flexible, highly customized interfaces.

**Kadence/Kadence Pro** is used when its lightweight and performance-focused approach is the better fit.

**Custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript** is added where a specific interaction, animation, or section needs more control.

**AI** supports research, ideation, prototyping, development, and experimentation — while the final implementation is reviewed and structured for the actual project requirements.
